Aux insert pool

Auxes the app may use for outboard units (it picks a free one on each insert). Untick an Aux you use for something else. Each Aux N in use needs User In N for its return, so keep your channels off those User Ins.

Relay for mixing apps

The Pi acts as the console for X32-Mix, Mixing Station, X32-Edit and other apps. The X32 talks only to the Pi. Point the apps at the Pi's IP (or use their console search). Turn it off to connect apps straight to the console again.
